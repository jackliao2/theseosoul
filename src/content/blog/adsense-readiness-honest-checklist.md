AdSense approval is a **review**, not a unit test. Tools can tell you whether the obvious trust and content signals are present. They cannot promise approval, bypass policy, or magically turn a thin affiliate brochure into a publisher.

This checklist is how we think about readiness at TheSeoSoul — aligned with our free [AdSense Readiness Checker](/tools/adsense-readiness-checker).

## What reviewers are roughly looking for

In plain language:

- A site that looks finished for humans  
- Original content with substance  
- Clear ownership and contact paths  
- Navigation that works  
- Policy pages that exist and match the product  
- No obvious violations (copyright scrapes, prohibited niches, deceptive ads layouts *before* you even have ads)

If your entire site is five pages of spun “best VPN” blurbs, no meta tag tweak will save the review.

## Trust pages that should exist

At minimum for most sites:

- **About** — who you are, why the site exists  
- **Contact** — working email or form (`hello@…` is fine; make it monitored)  
- **Privacy** — especially if you use analytics or plan personalized ads  
- **Terms** or equivalent — when you sell something or run accounts  

Empty “lorem privacy” templates are worse than a short honest policy. Write like a human.

## Content sample quality

Before you apply:

- Ship enough articles or product pages that the site has a clear topic  
- Prefer depth over a burst of 400-word outlines  
- Make titles and intros match the page  
- Avoid scraped or spun libraries  

Use the readiness checker to sample whether key pages look substantial. Use your judgment for originality — machines are bad at “did you rip this from Wikipedia?”

## Technical hygiene that still matters

AdSense is not Search Console, but broken sites fail both:

- HTTPS with valid TLS — [SSL Days Checker](/tools/ssl-checker)  
- Pages indexable when you want traffic — [Noindex Checker](/tools/noindex-checker)  
- Crawlable robots — [Robots.txt Checker](/tools/robots-txt-checker)  
- Sane titles/descriptions — [Meta Tag Checker](/tools/meta-tag-checker)  

A [full technical audit](/#home-audit-url) is a useful pre-flight for the same reasons you’d run it before a relaunch.

## Navigation and UX

- Working menu to cornerstone sections  
- Mobile layouts that do not hide all content behind broken drawers  
- No forced downloads or aggressive interstitial spam  
- Language consistency (don’t mix random auto-translated stubs)

## What our checker does (and does not)

**Does:** fetch public pages, look for trust URLs, sample content signals, surface gaps you can fix today.  

**Does not:** submit to Google, predict approval odds as a percentage, or replace [Google’s publisher policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/48182).

If anyone sells “guaranteed AdSense approval,” walk away.

## Application day

1. Run the [AdSense Readiness Checker](/tools/adsense-readiness-checker).  
2. Fix missing About/Contact/Privacy.  
3. Publish or prune thin stubs.  
4. Confirm HTTPS and indexability on the homepage.  
5. Apply when *you* would trust the site as a reader — not when a dashboard turns green.

Approval still takes patience. Use the waiting time to write one more genuinely useful page. That habit compounds for SEO and GEO long after the ads snippet appears.
